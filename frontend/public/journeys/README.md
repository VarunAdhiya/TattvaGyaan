# Divine Journey illustrations

Created with OpenAI image generation for this feature on 2026-10-20.
These are original, artistic 3D-style raster illustrations, not scanned scripture
images, photographs of historical events, or interactive 3D models.

- `ganesha.png`: Ganesha at a Himalayan temple garden, with mouse and lotus pond.
- `birth.png`: Parvati with human-headed child Ganesha before his transformation.
- `threshold.png`: human-headed Ganesha at the doorway with Shiva and Nandi.
- `family.png`: elephant-headed Ganesha with Shiva, Parvati and a peacock.
- `scribe.png`: Ganesha and Vyasa in a forest hermitage.
- `river.png`: Ganesh Chaturthi at river ghats.
- `shiva.png`, `krishna.png`, `hanuman.png`: deity introduction scenes.

The saved “AI Spiritual Platform Guide.html” was inspected for visual references.
Its third-party thumbnails were not republished. The portfolio reference inspired
the idea of visiting successive places; none of its artwork or source was copied.

Story provenance is attached to each chapter in `src/data/divineJourneys.js`.
The interface explicitly labels narrative variation and artistic interpretation.
