# Google sign-in assets

`google-sign-in-dark.svg` is Google's unmodified dark square icon button asset:
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/branding_guideline_sample_dk_sq_sl.svg

The login and registration pages retain this icon with its original dark backing.
At the user's request, the surrounding button matches the email fields with a
translucent brown fill (`#281b16b3`), cream text, and a warm border. Keyboard focus
and existing OAuth behavior are preserved. This custom surrounding fill differs
from Google's standard dark button fill.

Reference: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines
